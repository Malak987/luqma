What changed

  • Header — one full-bleed band from the very top edge, painted behind the status bar, with only its bottom corners rounded (home_header): wordmark + favourites (count badge) + notifications (unread dot), the greeting with a short headline under it, the tappable address plate (home_app_bar_address) and the search field with a clear action. No margins, no gaps, no floating card.
  • Categories — card boxes, gradients and shadows removed. A flat cream circle holds new transparent vector art, with the bold name under it. Keys kept: category_card_<id>, category_icon_background_<id>, home_categories_rail.
  • Promo — flat burgundy banner with a gold badge and CTA, no gradient, no rings, no invented “50%”. Multiple ads become a swipeable carousel with quiet indicators that follows RTL; with no ads the default banner stays.
  • Restaurant cards — the cover is strictly images[0] (BoxFit.cover), then iconUrl, then the Luqma placeholder. The gallery is never scanned for another photo. The logo badge (iconUrl) appears whenever the cover is a photo, even when the two URLs are identical — the slots are independent. Zero values are omitted instead of rendered: no 0 min, no Free, no 0 km, no rating when the count is 0, and isOpenNow == null stays unknown.
  • Address — PUT /api/Address/Update/{id} wired through data source → repository → use case → AddressFormCubit (edit mode keeps label, isDefault and the stored coordinates). The saved record updates the header immediately.
